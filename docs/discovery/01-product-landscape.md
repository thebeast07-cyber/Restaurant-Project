# 01 - Product Landscape

## Executive Summary
Majoo is a cloud-based Software as a Service (SaaS) Business Management / ERP platform tailored for Indonesian Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs/UMKM). It provides an all-in-one ecosystem bridging front-office operations (POS, payments) with back-office administration (Inventory, HR, CRM, Accounting, Omnichannel).

## Target Customer & Segmentation
- **Segment:** Primarily MSMEs (UMKM), scaling up to mid-sized franchises and enterprises.
- **Business Categories:** Food & Beverage (Restaurants, Cafes, Kiosks), Retail (Grocery, Fashion, Electronics), Services (Salons, Clinics, Car Washes), and Pharmacies.

## Product Positioning
Majoo positions itself as an integrated ecosystem ("Aplikasi Wirausaha Lengkap"). Instead of using disparate systems for POS, HR, and Accounting, merchants use Majoo as a single source of truth for their entire operation.

## Subscription Tiers & Packaging
Majoo operates on a per-outlet, per-month SaaS subscription model. 

| Tier | Price (approx) | Target | Core Inclusions |
|------|---------------|--------|-----------------|
| **Starter** | Rp 249.000 / month / outlet | Single-outlet MSMEs | Basic POS, Inventory, Basic CRM, Web Store. |
| **Advance** | Rp 499.000 / month / outlet | Growing/Multi-outlet | Adds Multi-branch management, full accounting, business analytics, EDC integration. |
| **Prime** | Rp 999.000 / month / outlet | Established businesses | Adds premium automation, advanced access controls (e.g., hiding COGS from staff). |
| **Prime+** | Custom Pricing | Enterprise | Custom features, Recipe Variants for F&B. |

## Ecosystem Capabilities

### Hardware Services
Majoo is hardware agnostic (Android, iOS, Windows, macOS) but also sells proprietary bundled hardware ("Majoo Supplies") including:
- POS Tablets & Standees (Compact, Neo)
- Bluetooth/LAN Receipt Printers
- Cash Drawers & Barcode Scanners
- Kitchen Display Systems (KDS)

### Payment Services (majoo Pay)
An integrated payment gateway that aggregates QRIS, E-wallets (GoPay, OVO, ShopeePay), and Cards (GPN, Visa, Mastercard) directly into the POS to enable auto-settlement and automated reconciliation.

### Managed Services
- **Majoo Accounting Services:** Merchants can hire Majoo's professional accountants to handle tax reporting and journal adjustments.

### Integration Ecosystem
Majoo features an Open API and native integrations with:
- Marketplace aggregators (Tokopedia, Shopee).
- Food delivery platforms (GrabFood, GoFood).
- External accounting tools (Xero, Jurnal).
- Specialized government systems (SATUSEHAT for healthcare).
